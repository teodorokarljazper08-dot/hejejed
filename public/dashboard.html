Proxies
What are Proxies?
A proxy is a middleman server that your bot connects through instead of connecting directly to Telegram. This hides your bot's real IP address and helps avoid rate limits or bans from Telegram, especially if you are running multiple bots.
Without Proxy
Your Bot → Telegram
Telegram sees your server IP. If many bots share the same IP, Telegram may rate-limit or ban it.
With Proxy
Your Bot → Proxy → Telegram
Each bot gets its own IP. More stable, less likely to get blocked by Telegram.
How to enable a proxy for your bot
1. Scroll down to Use Per Bot and toggle the proxy switch for your bot.
2. Restart the bot — it will now route traffic through the proxy automatically.
3. You do not need to change anything in your code. KARL handles the proxy setup.
Proxies are shared from the KARL pool or assigned to you personally by the admin. If you need more or a dedicated proxy, use the Request More section below.

Environment Variables
What are Environment Variables?
Environment variables let you store sensitive information like API keys, passwords, and tokens outside your code. Instead of hardcoding your bot token inside your Python file, you save it here and your bot reads it automatically when it starts.
This keeps your credentials safe — if you share your bot code with someone, they won't see your keys because they aren't in the code itself.
Example — Bot Token
Key              Value
BOT_TOKEN      8554648666:AAF0gTJT6cfPuN6rq...
ADMIN_ID       123456789
API_KEY        sk-abc123yourkeyhere
In your Python code, read them like this:
# At the top of your bot.py
import os
BOT_TOKEN = os.environ.get("BOT_TOKEN")
ADMIN_ID  = os.environ.get("ADMIN_ID")
API_KEY   = os.environ.get("API_KEY")
After saving variables here, restart your bot for them to take effect. Variables are applied every time your bot starts.
